Directions for a Nordic, technical stock desk

Each frame is a working HTML mockup that shows real Yahoo Finance data for 12 US stocks. Each one covers the same four jobs: scan the watchlist, check the portfolio, compare up to four stocks and dig into one. They differ in what they put first.

  • Prices: US close 17 Nov 2026
  • Fetched 18 Nov, 13:34 Helsinki
  • Desk frames: example portfolio
  • Impact frame: example portfolio, invented values

Huurre

Finnish for hoarfrost

A light, dense desk. The watchlist is a ledger: every row shows the price, today’s move, a 52-week range rule and a three-month trace. Click a row and the stock opens on the right.

Best at
Daily scanning. Everything is on one screen with no scrolling.
Trade-off
The quietest of the three. It impresses through precision, not spectacle.
Also shows
The watchlist in its loading, empty and error states.
Open Huurre

Kaamos

Finnish for the polar night

A dark instrument screen built around a momentum map. Each stock sits by its 6-month return (across) and 1-month return (up), with SPY as the crosshair. Your holdings are ringed, so you see at a glance which ones are falling behind.

Best at
Seeing the whole watchlist as one picture and spotting turns.
Trade-off
The map needs a sentence of explanation the first time. It is the strongest piece to show a recruiter.
Open Kaamos

Aamu

Finnish for morning

A printed-style morning report for someone who wakes up in Helsinki after New York has closed. The euro value is set large, one sentence says how you did against SPY, and twelve small charts share one scale.

Best at
A two-minute read with coffee. Currency effect shown separately.
Trade-off
It reads top to bottom, so it is slower to operate than a desk.
Open Aamu

Impact

an example portfolio, in the Huurre style

What the metrics say about a portfolio, and what changes if you add a stock or sell a holding. Each holding shows its share of the money next to its share of the risk. Pick a stock and an amount to see volatility, beta, worst fall and concentration before and after.

Best at
Deciding what a new buy does to the whole portfolio, not just whether the stock looks good.
Reads as
The past year replayed with today’s weights. It describes the past, not a forecast.
Open Impact

Shared by all three

  • Times in Helsinki: the US close falls at 23:00 and New York opens at 16:30.
  • The portfolio is valued in euros, and the currency effect is shown apart from the stock returns.
  • Return, volatility, beta and worst fall are computed from daily closes. Valuation fields come from Yahoo.
  • The compare panel rebases each stock to 100 one year ago, against SPY.
  • The selected stock and the compare set live in the page link, so a view can be bookmarked.
